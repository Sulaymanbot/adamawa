Help

Everything you need in one minute.

Register

  • Use made-up details. Never your real voter card or NIN.
  • Pick your LGA, then ward, then polling unit.
  • Wait for an officer to verify you.

Vote

  • Sign in and press Go to ballot.
  • Pick one candidate, check it, then submit.
  • A vote can't be changed once submitted.
  • Keep your receipt code. It doesn't show your choice.

Limitations

  • This is a university demo, not an official election system.
  • Votes are encrypted, but this alone doesn't fully guarantee secrecy or verifiability.
  • All people, places and results are fictional.
